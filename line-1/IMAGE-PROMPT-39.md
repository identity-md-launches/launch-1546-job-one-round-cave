# The Rooted Seed

Used the installed imagegen skill with the built-in image generation tool. Exact
prompt:

> Use case: illustration-story. Generate a single compact prehistoric cave-painting pigment motif isolated on pure white, for adding to an existing ancestral cave wall without redrawing its rock or older marks. The motif is one small irregular red ochre seed or pebble at the top, attached to a crooked charcoal stem descending to a root with two uneven side branches, each ending in a tiny dark pebble. It represents tracing a true seed through its rooted branching path. Extremely simple Lascaux burnt-stick and thumb marks, rough broken grain, worn rubbed earth pigment, flat, handmade uneven strokes. Center the small motif with generous white margin. Earth pigments only: charcoal black and red ochre, no green. No letters, numbers, captions, logos, signatures, watermarks, borders, frame, hands, handprints, people, photographs, 3D, or shadows. Output actual image bytes; this is an insert for preserving the existing cave.
